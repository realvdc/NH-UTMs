Create clean, consistent campaign URLs for GA4 reporting. Pick a channel preset, fill in the rest, and copy a link that tracks correctly every time.

Quick guide

  • Pick a channel preset first — it auto-fills source, medium, and campaign for that platform.
  • Keep values lowercase and consistent so GA4 groups the same channel together instead of splitting it into duplicates.
  • Use term and content only when the channel calls for it — ad variant, audience segment, or post ID.
  • Click Copy URL and paste directly into your campaign platform — no need to double-check the raw string.
